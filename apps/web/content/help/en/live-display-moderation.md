Open the event's **Live Display** tab. The **On screen** section is where you control what the displays and the Sharing Station show.

## Publishing: automatic or approved first

Under **Publishing** choose:

- **Show automatically.** A capture goes on screen as soon as its files have reached the server, usually a minute or two after the guest's spin.
- **Approve first.** New captures wait in your queue until you show them. Use it when you can't risk an unflattering or inappropriate moment on a big screen.

Changing this affects captures from then on. Screens pick up the change within a second.

## The three lists

| List               | What is in it                                                                    |
| ------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Waiting (3)**    | Captures that need your approval. Only captures whose files have arrived appear. |
| **On screen (12)** | What displays and the Sharing Station are showing.                               |
| **Hidden (1)**     | Captures you took off the screens.                                               |

Each capture appears with a thumbnail and its code. The buttons:

- **Show on screen** puts a waiting capture up.
- **Hide from screens** removes a capture at once from every display and station.
- **Show again** puts a hidden capture back.

## What hiding does and doesn't do

Hiding a capture takes it off the screens and out of the **event gallery** that guests can open. It does not delete it: the guest who made it still has their own link, and **Download every file (ZIP)** in the Studio still includes it. If a capture needs to disappear entirely, the guest can delete it from their page, or you can erase it by following [Guest privacy](help:guest-privacy).

## Guests who opt out

When Live Display is on, the result screen tells each guest: **Your capture may appear on the event's big screen.** They can press **Keep mine off the screen**, and then it never goes up, whether publishing is automatic or approved. You can't bring such a capture back; the choice is theirs.

## If you turn Live Display off

With **Show captures on screens** off, displays and the Sharing Station go back to a calm idle screen; no captures are shown. Nothing is deleted. Turn it on again and the captures that were on screen return.
