---
title: Do I need internet at the venue?
summary: Not to run the booth. Guests get their files once the device is online again.
category: faq
order: 30
keywords: [internet, wifi, offline, connection, hotspot, venue, 4g, upload]
updated: 2026-10-28

Not to capture. The booth records, renders and shows each guest a working QR code with no connection at all, and uploads in the background when it can. Guests can download their files as soon as the upload has finished: with a good connection that is a minute or two after their capture.

The device does need to be online once beforehand so it can load the app and the event's files, which the [setup check](help:setup-check) does for you. A phone hotspot is a fine backup. Before you leave, check the booth says nothing is waiting to upload. The details are in [Offline mode and the upload queue](help:offline-mode-and-uploads).
