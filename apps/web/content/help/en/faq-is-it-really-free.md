Yes. Every feature is free: no paywall, no watermark on photos or videos, and no fee per device or per event. You can register as many devices as you like.

It stays free because costs are kept down by design: videos are rendered on your own device instead of a server, hosted guest files expire after 7, 30 or 90 days, files are stored somewhere that doesn't charge for downloads, and you can send copies to your own Google Drive or Dropbox for keeps.

What you do provide yourself is the hardware and anything around it: a camera and a device for each booth, a printer with its paper and ink, and the venue's internet. See [Which device should I use?](help:faq-which-device) and [How long guest files are kept](help:expiry-and-extending).
