## Before the day

1. **Sign in.** Go to **Sign in**, type your email and press **Email me a sign-in link**. The link works for 10 minutes. You can also use **Continue with Google**. There is no password, and guests never need an account.
2. **Create an event.** On **Home**, pick **360 Booth** or **Photo Booth**, then give the event a name and a date. Choose how long guest files are kept: 7, 30 or 90 days (30 by default). You can change everything later.
3. **Set it up.** The **Setup** tab holds the event's settings: spin length and frame rate for a 360 booth, number of photos and printing for a photo booth, how guests receive their files, and the kiosk PIN. Changes save by themselves; **All changes saved** tells you when they have.
4. **Make it yours.** The **Branding** tab sets the colours, logo, start screen wording, print layout or video overlay, intro and outro, music and looks.
5. **Register the booth device.** The first time a device opens a booth it asks you to register it. Give it a name you will recognise at the venue, such as _Booth A iPad_. See [Devices and registration](help:devices-and-registration).
6. **Run the setup check.** On the event page press **Setup check**. It tests the camera, storage, a short recording, render speed, a QR scan and uploads, and tells you what to fix. See [Run the setup check](help:setup-check).

> [!TIP]
> Do steps 5 and 6 at home or at the venue the day before, on the same device and the same Wi-Fi you will use at the event. They take about ten minutes and catch nearly every problem.

## At the venue

1. Open the event and press **Start booth**. Check the picture, then hand the device to your guests.
2. A guest taps the start button (or presses a key on a remote, or just starts spinning if you turned on motion start).
3. The booth records, saves the take on the device, and shows the guest a QR code straight away, with a six-character code for anyone who cannot scan. The video follows in a minute or two while the device renders it in the background.
4. Press **Next guest** for the next person. The booth is ready again at once.

## After the event

- The event page shows how many captures there are, what is still rendering or uploading, and when guest links expire.
- Share the **Event gallery** link, or print a poster from the **QR signage** tab.
- To keep every original, press **Download every file (ZIP)**. See [Gallery and ZIP export](help:gallery-and-zip-export).

> [!NOTE]
> Recordings are saved on the device first and uploaded afterwards, so a dropped connection does not lose anything. See [Offline mode and uploads](help:offline-mode-and-uploads).
