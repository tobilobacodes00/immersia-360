There are four ways back to a guest's files:

1. **Their code.** Every capture has a six-character code, like `K7M-2QX`. It is shown under the QR code on the booth. The guest types it at the address on the booth's result screen (this site's `/c` page, or your short domain) and arrives at their page.
2. **The event gallery.** Anyone with the gallery link can type the code in **Find by code**, or just look through the captures. If you set a password, they need it.
3. **The Sharing Station.** If you run one, they find their capture and scan its QR code.
4. **The booth.** In the booth's **Gallery**, open the capture: its **Reshare** QR code can be scanned again. That works on the booth device, so you do it for them.

If their capture was hidden from the screens, it is not in the gallery or the Sharing Station, but its link and code still work. See [How guests get their photos and videos](help:sharing-with-guests).
