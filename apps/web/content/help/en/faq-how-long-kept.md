title: How long do guests have to download their files?

For the period you set on the event under **Keep guest files for**: 7, 30 or 90 days, 30 by default. Each guest's page shows the date their files will be removed. Before then you can press **Extend by 7 days** or **Extend by 30 days** on the event's Overview. After the files are gone they can't be brought back.

To keep files for good, copy them to your own Google Drive or Dropbox as they are made, or download the whole event as a ZIP. See [How long guest files are kept, and extending them](help:expiry-and-extending).
