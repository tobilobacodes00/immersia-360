Any recent laptop, tablet or phone with a current browser can run a booth. Three things decide how well:

- **The camera.** For a 360 booth with slow motion, a camera that really delivers 60 fps at 1080p matters more than the device. A tablet's own camera or a good webcam is enough; a phone's back camera is better still. Pick the wide lens so the whole platform is in frame.
- **Render speed.** Videos are made on the device. A 15 second, 1080p clip should render in 45 seconds or less on a laptop, and 90 seconds or less on a 2021 iPad. The [setup check](help:setup-check) measures your device.
- **Free storage.** Keep at least 2 GB free, plus room for a long day of recordings.

Keep the device on power, and mount it so it can't be knocked. For an iPad or iPhone, add the booth to the Home Screen first: see [iPad and iPhone storage](help:ios-storage-eviction).
