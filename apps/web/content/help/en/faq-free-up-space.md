**Storage low** means under 2 GB is free on the device. Open the booth's **Render queue** and **Delete** takes you no longer need: that removes the take and what was rendered from it from the device, and files already uploaded are not affected. First make sure the pills show nothing is waiting to upload, because a take that hasn't uploaded exists only on this device.

Then free space the usual way: remove other apps, photos and videos, and empty any recently deleted folder. The [setup check](help:setup-check) reports the free space. More in [iPad and iPhone storage](help:ios-storage-eviction) and [Offline mode and the upload queue](help:offline-mode-and-uploads).
