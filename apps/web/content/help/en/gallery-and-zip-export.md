## The event gallery

Every event has a gallery: one page with all the captures guests can see, for your client and for guests who didn't get their QR code. Find its link on the event's **Overview** tab, under **Event gallery**. Press **Copy** to copy it, or **Open** to see it.

On the gallery page anyone with the link can:

- filter by **All**, **360 videos** or **Photos** if the event runs both booths;
- **find by code** by typing the six-character code from the booth;
- open a capture, and **Download all** as a ZIP. The ZIP is built in their browser from signed links; the server never zips anything.

Captures you hid from the screens, and those whose guests asked to be kept off the screen, never appear in the gallery. See [Moderate what is on screen](help:live-display-moderation).

### Protect it with a password

Under **Password**, press **Set a password** (at least 6 characters) and **Set**. A visitor types it once per browser, for a week. Changing the password signs everyone out. **Remove password** makes the gallery open again to anyone with the link. After too many wrong tries from one network, the gallery asks the visitor to wait a few minutes.

## QR signage {#signage}

The **QR signage** tab makes a poster for the whole event gallery, in your event's colours, with your logo and hashtag:

| Format         | Size             | For                                                                          |
| -------------- | ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **A4 poster**  | 210 × 297 mm     | A wall, a door or an easel.                                                  |
| **A5 flyer**   | 148 × 210 mm     | Tables, hand-outs and the bar.                                               |
| **Table tent** | A4 sheet, folded | Print, fold on the dashed line, stand on the table: both sides read upright. |

Change the headline and the line under the code, or leave a line empty to drop it. Download a **PDF** or a **PNG**. Print at **actual size (100%)**, not "fit to page". Nothing is placed closer than 12 mm to the edge, so office printers won't cut it off.

## Download every original as a ZIP {#zip}

On the **Overview** tab, **Event gallery** has a section **All files** with **Download every file (ZIP)**. It saves every full-quality file of the event, **including hidden captures**, straight from storage into one ZIP in your browser.

- In **Chrome and Edge** on a computer, the ZIP is written straight to the file you choose, so even a multi-gigabyte event doesn't sit in memory.
- Other browsers build the ZIP in memory first. That can fail for a very large event, so use Chrome or Edge on a computer for the full export.
- A file that has expired or is missing is skipped and counted; the rest of the ZIP is saved. You can cancel at any time, and nothing is saved.
- Export before the files expire. See [Expiry and extending](help:expiry-and-extending). Or let Immersia copy every file to your own cloud as it is made: [Connect Google Drive](help:connect-google-drive).

Owners, operators and viewers can all export a gallery. Hosted guest files are deleted after the event's retention period; the ZIP and your cloud copies are yours to keep.

## From the booth itself

The booth has its own **Gallery** (the pictures button in its top bar): every video and strip made on this device, filterable by type, with its upload state. Select some, or all, and press **Export** to save them as a ZIP; **Import originals** adds clips from disk; a capture's detail view has a **Reshare** QR code for a guest who missed theirs. Exports and imports wait while a guest is at the booth.
